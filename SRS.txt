
The system is standalone and modular, consisting of multiple management modules such as:

1)Movie Management
2)Showtime Management
3)Customer Management
4)Ticket Booking
5)Booking History

All modules are interrelated and share common data (like `movieId` and `showId`).

Product Functions

1. **Movie Management

   * Add new movies
   * View all movies
   * Each movie has: `movieId`, `title`, `genre`, `duration`, `language`

2. Showtime Management

   * Add showtimes for movies
   * View all showtimes
   * Each showtime has: `showId`, `movieId`, `date`, `time`

3. Customer Management

   * Add customer details
   * View customer list
   * Each customer has: `customerId`, `name`, `email`, `phone`

4. Ticket Booking

   * Book tickets for a specific customer and showtime
   * View all booked tickets
   * Each ticket has: `ticketId`, `customerId`, `showId`, `numberOfSeats`

5. Booking History
   * View all past bookings by a customer
